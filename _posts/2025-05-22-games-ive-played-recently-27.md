After some more procrastination on writing this, here's **Part 2** of **Games I've Played Recently**. There are some weird games in this one:

- **Spaceplan:** This is a mostly standard incremental game. You are a probe. Send some potato satellites to generate some energy. Time travel. Discover a potato civilization. Break space-time. All the fun stuff. I played this game waaay back, but played it again recently. It's just some chill stupid fun watching numbers go up. There are some skill challenges too, something you don't often see in incremental clickers.
- **Scanner Sombre:** This is a weird one. You are in a super dark cave with a laser scanner. The only way to see is to mark the area with the dots from your scanner. Of course, that lends itself super well to horror. Discover some paranormal cult stuff in this dark cave and find a dark truth. This game has some pretty boring segments because your character walks slowly, but it's interesting. I actually played this again (to the end this time) because I was prototyping a game with laser dot vision myself, but it was fun nonetheless.
- **7 Days to End With You:** This is the second weird one. In this visual novel you wake up with no memories. There is this girl that seems to be helping you out, but you can't understand her because you forgot your vocabulary. Oh and you die after 7 in game days. Ask her questions, walk around, figure out what this weird language means. You can figure out a lot of meaning from simple things, but unfortunately the game requires you to go in full cipher mode to understand -everything-. But I understood like 90% without a guide then looked up a guide to understand even more! I really should play Chants of Sennaar.
- **Mechabellum:** Another game I played waaay back and I'm playing again. You play multiplayer battles in rounds. Each round you place all sorts of robots on your battlefield, upgrade them, get new ones. Then your units battle the enemy automatically. Get more resources, rinse and repeat until one of you loses enough health. I don't like playing multiplayer games alone, so I'm basically only playing this because my brother likes it a lot and you can play 2v2. It's fun enough for that though. And it being turn based means you can chat in-between rounds.

And that was part 2. I have even weirder games for the next parts. I've been playing some very weird games recently lol. But until then, happy rest of your week once again!
