Once again after a long "break" that took forever (its 2026 already?) I bring you another Games I've Played Recently Part 1! This time I'll go in reverse chronological order because I sorta want to talk about this last game I played:

- **Consume Me:** This is a little indie game that says it's about dieting but really it's about the stress of being a student in the modern times. You balance personal goals, academic goals, relationship goals, all while trying to keep to a diet. To me this was an interesting one, because I played it so well the game was like "how did you do all this? did you play this before? Anyways you aren't supposed to do all those successfully so here's how everything fell apart regardless", which is accurate to real life. Sometimes you can do everything in your overly ambitious to-do list, and still find out none of it matters.
- **Drill Bird:** A tiny indie game about a bird who drills. I love drilling/upgrades games so this was a fun evening. I do wish the game was longer, but with its limited length it still brings some interesting mechanics to the genre. And the bird is cute.
- **Feed the Reactor:** Another tiny game about generating power with a reactor. I also love whenever a little game brings something new to a genre I love, and this one plays with physics+incremental gameplay. It doesn't have a lot of moving pieces, but the existing pieces all interact in interesting ways, so totally recommended for a fun evening.
- **Death Stranding:** Wow there is a lot to say about this one. I was playing this as the weather was shifting between hot and cold, and I was shifting between knowing what to do and not. Death Stranding is at its core about a lonely journey to bring people together. Let's first talk about the gameplay. It was really interesting to see how Death Stranding's main gameplay is about doing something that's usually boring: walking from place A to place B. But they managed to add surprising depth to walking. How much cargo will you take? How much gear will you take to deal with unknowns on the road? How many tasks will you attempt at once, or will you take your time? The idea to make other player's structures also exist in the world was also fun. Seeing how other players had the same idea as you to take a very unreasonable route and place some helping structures on the way was fun. The story itself was confusing at times, I'm not sure if I fully understood it. But it has sooo much in there that I think everyone will take something out of it. I'm waiting excitedly for Death Stranding 2 to come out on PC to continue this journey. But this is also a game where I think I played it a bit too well. Vehicles are a bit too powerful, and the later unlocked ziplines let you skip a lot of the difficulties. Oh well, maybe the balance is better in the next one.

And that's it for this week! I have enough games to make many more parts, so look forward to those in the upcoming weeks!
