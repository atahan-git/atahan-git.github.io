Life has been hectic for me for the past few months but I managed to play some games nevertheless! There are too many games again so I will split this into two. So here's another Games I've Played Sometime Recently Part 1:

- **Rogue Voltage:** This is a nice little roguelike where the main gimmick is you "wire up" different components in your backpack to shoot or heal or do other things. Eg wire the red wire of the generator to the booster, which has a purple wire output, which connects to your gun that also has only purple wire as input. This was nice for a bit, but the game didn't do much with its unique concept (eg nothing relationally affects anything, and things don't actually "run", the game is turn based so you send pulses of electricity) so it didn't grab me for very long. Still nice to know that even a small exploration of a concept (with a nice coat of paint) can bring joy for people! At least for a few hours.
- **Dark and Darker:** I wanted to like this game but couldn't. This is a PvPvE random dungeon crawler where you get dropped into a dungeon with some other players and have to get loot, kill monsters, then escape through stairs. The dungeon's layout doesn't change so you are meant to learn your way around. This game could've been nice, but it suffers from the usual "you joined the game too late so now every player is a pro and you get instantly killed by other players before you can do anything". Soooo yeah. Not too much fun to do that over and over while trying to learn the game.
- **Selaco:** I wanted to like this game too but again a hard sell. I've been following this game's development for a bit and I was excited for it because it sells itself as an "old school shooter" similar to Half Life. And Half Life is great. But Selaco falls flat similar to how I didn't like Black Mesa as much as I liked the original Half Life. There is so much clutter everywhere! It's so hard to see what is a functional piece of the environment and what is just decoration. But also I'll definitely come back to this after some years of tweaking and them finishing developing the full story so maybe they'll fix it! Or maybe it's just not for me.
- **Infection Free Zone:** This is a fairly standard top down make a village, loot places, and survive the zombie apocalypse game but with a unique twist: you play in a map of the real world! It was surprisingly fun to make a stronghold in various places I've lived over the years. The overall vibes of the game also change when you place your base in an american suburb vs in the middle of a densely populated city so that was fun too. Other than the real life maps though, the game is fairly simple. It does have an interesting enough story to keep you going for a bit though!
- **Witch Hand:** This was also a game I've been following for a while and recently it was on sale! This one is a "stacklands like" where you have a board and cards. You combine cards, eg crystal mine + witch + 20 seconds = crystal, or 3 crystal + house spell = house, to progress through the game. This one was different from Stacklands with its witch aesthetic but also by making the witch your focal point. The game even has a few different witches to pick from that change the gameplay to warrant some replayability. I loved the original Stacklands so this was a joy to play too! Lots of mysteries to discover and a demon lord to beat at the end too. The stacklands formula seems simple on the surface but there are very few games that use it, probably because it's a fairly niche genre with few players. I'm excited to see what new games come out in the same genre, and I might even make one myself someday!
