As promised **Part 2** of my **Games I've Played Recently**:

- **Dune Imperium:** This is probably my favorite board game, so I was a bit skeptical in trying out the videogame version of it. Dune Imperium is a deck building, worker placement game where you fight for control of Arrakis against 3 of your friends. The videogame works basically the same as the board game, but they also added great AI for when you can't fill the game with people. I love playing this with my brother every other week. The game is deep enough that when you have nothing to talk about it can occupy 100% of your brain, but also they have added enough randomness/cool stuff to do in the game that even when you are only paying 5% attention (and having great conversation in the meantime!) you can have a lot of fun! It is also great that there is a lot of skill in the game, but with smaller skill differences win rates get close really quickly, so everyone can have fun! And you get to do cool stuff/side objectives even when losing so it works out.
- **Factorio Space Age DLC:** This was the big game I've been playing and I wanted to finish before writing this out. Factorio is my favorite game of all time, and I've been waiting for this DLC basically ever since they announced it a couple years ago. In Factorio you build factories, and playing the game feels a lot like writing code, but without all the annoying parts of writing actual code. In Space Age DLC they amazed me all over again by adding sooo many new unique spins to the existing mechanics. Modding scene has always been big in Factorio, and over the years I have played most of the big mods, so many people were worried the DLC would be lackluster compared to all the amazing mods we got. But the devs really proved their expertise once again by basically adding 5 massive mod sized additions to the game at the same time. They also gave the biggest honor to the many of the QoL mods by killing them: they added them to the game natively. I had a great time playing this over the past couple of weeks, and I'm excited what new mods we'll see that will use all the cool new mechanics they added to the game!
- **Chrono Ark:** This was a surprising hit game for me. In Chrono Ark you assemble a team of bigger than life anime characters and build a deck to fight against the "black fog" that drives animals and people crazy, all in your quest to get time shards to save your fellow investigators. The game has solid deckbuilder roguelite gameplay, so I definitely recommend it if you like Slay the Spire and such, but where it excels is in its story. You learn how its ensemble of characters became their exaggerated selves, Why you are in this weird loop, Why is this Azar character is acting all weird, What happened to humanity, What it means to be conscious, and What it means to sacrifice. The anime exterior will be off-putting to some people, but if thats your jam give it a go.

And that's all! In the upcoming weeks I'll probably do a mini version with **Half Life 2** as I'm playing through that listening to all the dev commentary. Or maybe I'll do another 3 month gap and have many more games all at once. Who knows!
