Here's Games I've Played Sometime Recently Part 2, as promised:

- **Drill Core Demo:** This was a demo, but I had more fun with it than a lot of other games I've played. In this Dome Keeper meets tower defense and unit management game you are drilling down on a platform. You mark places in a square grid for your little guys to dig for resources, which you then use to build things on your platform to boost your digging and other capabilities. Every couple minutes "night time" arrives where monsters attack you from the top where your towers defend you. I always love to see how a favorite game of mine (Dome Keeper) inspires other slightly different but a lot of fun games! I played a little too much of this demo trying to beat the hardest difficulty.
- **Stellar Initiative:** Another tight roguelike with tower defense this time. You fight some monsters in different levels with slightly different win conditions to earn resources and artifacts that boost your towers. You also get different "factions" with totally unique towers too. I found it surprising how many different towers and perks the developers got out of the simple gameplay. Surprisingly unique monsters too, you even have to fight invincible enemies! I had lots of fun (and some rage moments) figuring out the best perks to beat the hardest difficulties this game offers.
- **Cryptmaster:** This was a "unique" game that totally tries to be unique and does so... okay. In this metroidvania-ish game you only use a keyboard and fight by typing. You go around solving riddles by asking questions and guessing words with an AI dungeon master (eg "What's in the box?" "what does it taste like?" tastes metallic "what does it look like?" its like a flat disc "is it a shield?" correct!). The correct guesses award you letters for skills your heroes can use. Then in combat you type "slash" or "yell" or "meditate" and such to fight. The idea is super interesting, but I feel like there is too much busywork in the game and too little content. It gets boring super fast typing the same "slash" "yell" "lightning" combo over and over and the speed you unlock new skills is very slow, and the new skills you unlock don't combo too well, at least in the early game. I wish they made the earlier skills more interesting. There are hundreds of skills in this game so I'm sure it gets interesting later but it really should get interesting faster.
- **To the Core:** When I'm stressed I like playing incremental games to get some dopamine and this game was exactly that. I do always get amazed at how many different games can form from simple ideas though. In this game you have a drill, and you are trying to drill to the core of planets. While doing that you get resources to upgrade your drill, unlock new planets, and do all the incremental stuff. This game didn't do anything amazing, but there was enough choice involved in finding the optimal path of upgrades and also how to actually dig and where to dig etc. that it kept me playing until the end of the game.

More shall come, eventually! Probably in a couple more months as always.
