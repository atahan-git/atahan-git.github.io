I kinda forgot about making games I've played for a while because a lot of other things were happening and I didn't even feel like I was playing that many games. But some people reminded me that this exists in the Zwift Week and when I looked back I realized I did in fact played a good number of games good enough to share! I have 6 games again which is too much, so I'll split this in two parts again. Soooo the part 1 of The Games I've Played Last Month-ish:

- **Landfall Archives:** This is a free collection of prototypes from the developer Landfall, who makes quirky physics-based games. I liked this one because it has many prototypes for ideas me (and probably many other developers) probably had at some point in their lives. And all these ideas (like what if you could build a walking house in a game?) have some work put into them, but also show how much an idea needs before it becomes a proper game. It is also reassuring to see other devs have soo many abandoned great ideas, as I have quite a few myself <img class="slack-emoji" src="/images/slack-stuck_out_tongue-1C1D8B14B508.png" alt=":stuck_out_tongue:" />. It is generally known that it takes lots of abandoned prototypes to find a good game, but it is rare to see prototypes shared like this, so I wanted to bring this one up here.
- **Black Skylands:** This is an interesting case. Black Skylands is a game about flying your skyship around floating islands and then doing some quests on those islands in the semi-open world. I saw this game first quite a while ago when it was in early access but I decided to wait until the "full release" to play. So I bought this as it got released recently but interestingly enough as community also says this game was a LOT better in early access! The team made a post about it saying how they had to cut all sorts of fun features to finish the game, which is probably better than abandoning it outright but still sad. I played a bit of the "final version" but the dev team also made the early access easily available (through steam betas) so after a bit I switched to the early access and never looked back! Even the "unfinished" early access version is a whole 6-8 hour game, and it only feels "unfinished" like the last hour or so. They even have a fairly okay "final boss" that doesn't tie up the story but I can totally see it being a setup for a second game in different circumstances. In the "final release" they cut back a lot of the unique interesting mechanics like collecting different resources to upgrade your gear, having to defend places you conquered, and the guns having a quirky 4 slot attachment system. And in their place is a more linear hand-holdy story, and just upgrading the guns with more of the same currency. They even made it so that the previously unique resources now just get converted to upgrade money. Definitely sad to see the team couldn't finish their dream version of the game but also great that they are being (somewhat) open about it and committing for at least some sort of completion, instead of abandoning the game like many do on Steam.
- **HELLDIVERS 2:** I've had originally played the first game in this series before so it was interesting to see where they took the idea. The first game was mostly the same, run around the map with 4 people doing objectives, but the play space in that one was in 2d and everyone was stuck in the same screen. That added a lot more chaos and teamkills but somehow even when the second game is fully 3d and you have all the map to run around away from your teammates, they made the teamkill options a lot wider ranged and effective so the same chaos remains in the second game. But kinda like the first game, after you rise up the ranks and do the hardest difficulty a whole bunch of times, there is not much left to do in the game. So this is a nice "play until the end" game but it didn't hold my attention enough to become a forever live service game for me. I do still follow it though an will probably play more once they drop some big extra content (like the expected 3rd faction) and following the "story" is somewhat interesting too. Overall a really good time while it lasts!

And that's it for now! I do always play more games than I share here, but most aren't interesting enough to share, didn't hold my attention long enough to "finish", or don't feel correct to share publicly here lol. Sooo many great games out there, but only a finite time to play and write about all of them...
