It has been a long time yet again, but I'm back with another list of games. This time I had too many nice games so I will be splitting this edition into two. I will post the second part next week. But for now, New Games I've Played Sometime Recently (part 1):

- **Dyson Sphere Program:** I was playing this last time I made this and it was, okay? In DSP you are a mech trying to build factories on a couple of planets to build a dyson sphere to get enough energy to send "compute power" back to your homeland. I play Factorio a lot, so every factory game I play I look through the Factorio lens, and this is one of the games that I enjoyed a lot more after I stopped doing that and embraced what this game does uniquely. Especially towards the late game I had a lot of fun with this one covering a planet from ground level up to the skies with conveyor belts and production lines. It is very satisfying to slowly see the sun get covered with every little rocket you send and see the clockwork machinery slowly cover every planet.
- **(the) Gnorp Apologue:** This was a nice contained idle/puzzle game where you assign jobs to your increasing number of gnorps to mine a magical rock. I like seeing innovation in the idle game space, and I like this one even more because unlike most idle games this one has a clear end goal. You try different builds with your ability points, and the best build changes over the course of the game as you unlock different abilities.
- **Cobalt Core:** In this Slay the Spire-like you have a spaceship and in turn based combat you use cards to either shoot, move around to dodge your enemy's attacks, spawn drones, get shields, or do other abilities you do in card based deck builder games. This one was a game I played a lot on my steam deck while relaxing on a couch, or while travelling. The combat is interesting, and there are quite a lot of new things to try between making a crew of 3 pilots and selecting different ships. Even though its a roguelike this one has a meta ending that asks you to finish the game with different crew members 3 times per member so it did take me a bit too long to finish, but it was kinda worth it at the end, and nicer as I switched to playing on easy to make the effects of RNG kinder. It's crazy how much potential there is in the deckbuilder roguelike genre, and I'm excited to see what other spin I see on this next.
- **Vectorio:** Another Factorio-like with more emphasis on the tower defense aspect where you get resources, craft better resources, then build towers to defend your base as more building attracts stronger enemies. As with many Factorio-likes this one reminded me of how much effort Factorio put into Quality of Life and how seeing the same features missing in other games makes me want to play another Factorio mod instead of a different game...

And that's all for the first part! Even more factory games with some indie games sprinkled in. I'm also eyeing the new Helldivers 2 as I played the first game way before and liked it but who knows what I will play next. I often pick what to play on the spot as I read an article about a new or old game, or sometimes I just look through my backlog of games on Steam or my wishlist. Soo many great games out there with so little time and energy to play...
