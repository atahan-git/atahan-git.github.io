As promised here's the second part of the New Games I've Played Sometime Recently:

- **Palworld:** Very popular game. Fun with friends. This one has a lot of "tasks" to do, but after the early game I found lots of them to be more of chores and "more of the same" rather than interesting twists. Especially after unlocking some pal abilities and guns, and nothing tops the early game fox flamethrower ability... I might just drop this one, or keep playing a little bit with friends until the end. But even with that the time I had with it is fun, which was unexpected for this kind of "lets mix a lot of popular games together and hope for the best" kind of game.
- **Frostrain:** Another "deck" builder game where your deck is your train and you are trying to go to the "promised land" while a storm is coming after you. I got a lot of fun out of this free to play student project trying out all the different strategies and getting every achievement in this fairly short game. And this was a nice surprise to me because as I'm making a train game as my hobby I've been trying out any train game I see and most of them were not that good. It's always interesting when a random free/cheap indie game like this pops up.
- **Last Train Home:** Another train game I played since the last time. In this game you are the last train taking Czechoslovak Legionaries left in post-WW1 civil war Russia all the way through Moscow to the east to the sea because you can't cross through the war zone for the shorter route. In your journey as the leader of your soldiers you help people, listen to the newly forming Czech government's demands, get enough food and coal for the journey, upgrade your train, and make some tough decisions. I love historically inspired games as they teach something while you are having fun, and it was interesting to see the harshness of the war and both the good and bad of what people do in such trying times. The game also comes with an in game encyclopedia of sorts for the players who want to learn more about the real life events that inspired the fictional in game events. The game definitely also has some shortcomings like how towards the end I had so much supply that I couldn't carry it all, while the narrative was going "oh no we are so little on food and fuel I don't know how we will make it through the winter!". But overall it was a well made experience with a really good narrative to carry it through all the way through Siberia to the end.
- **Exit 8:** A short photorealistic game about trying to escape from a japanese metro tunnel where weird and semi-scary things happen. I spent like the first 30 minutes of this not having any idea what is happening before realizing how to play the game is written on a wall lol. And some of the "odd things happening" were pretty difficult to notice, really making you second guess your observations. In the end I caved in and read a guide to finally finish the game but even when knowing all the rules it was a unique experience to try to escape this tunnel.
- **Buckshot Roulette:** This game was really popular online a while back and I decided to give it a go. In this game you are playing a variation of roulette with a shotgun against an AI opponent. This game spices things up with a whole bunch of items to increase your odds of winning. This one has unique indie graphics, and it's a fun, short, contained game.

Aaand that was part 2. Some bigger games, and some smaller games.
