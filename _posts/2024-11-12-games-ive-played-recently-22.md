After a now normal months long time gap in between I finally gathered (and completed, this time completing took so long) another Games I've Played Recently! Like last time I have collected a few too many games so this one will also come in parts. Here's part 1:

- **Frontline Crisis:** I got this game as part of a bundle of 5 games (I wanted to only buy Swarm Grinder) but this game surprisingly ended up being the only fun game of the bunch. In Frontline Crisis you assemble a "building zone" using tetris like pieces, then build various buildings like mines or turrets while you go around with your tank collecting some items. Gameplay is split into fairly short levels so you build your base again and again. Not the best game, but was interesting enough for me for 2.1 hours.
- **Drill Core (early access release):** I was waiting for this game to release after having lots of fun with the demo in a Steam Next Fest half a year ago but I was a bit disappointed. In Drill Core you are drilling down on a platform by sending orders to your miners, and at night time you build turrets to defend your mining shaft from monsters coming from above. This is still a very fun game with a unique gameplay that mixes a bunch of other games but it seems the development team has no idea how to properly handle the meta progression between each drilling run to keep the game fresh and to shake things up. So if you ever want to see a fun game that suffers from meta progression give this game a go! Definitely a good one for the game design analysis.
- **Shapez 2:** I was uncertain about playing this one as I didn't like the original Shapez, the free browser game, that much. In Shapez 2 (and also in Shapez 1) you create complex shapes from more basic ones. Get a circle, cut it in half, replace the cut out part with a square you painted blue, then stack it on top of another shape, and tie all these steps by building conveyors between little machines. My factory game enthusiast friends recommended this a lot, so I gave it a go. Then I was disappointed again. But then as I shared my pain points my friends recommended me a totally new way to play this game. Then as I started playing it in a more relaxed "wasteful" way as my friends recommended I started having lots of fun! Give this a go if you want to get into factory games but find the usual ones intimidating. Shapez 2 has no enemies, everything is free to build, and steps to make things are straightforward.
- **Frostpunk 2:** I had a lot of high hopes for this game because Frostpunk 1 is one of my favorite games of all time, and this game met -some- of those hopes. In Frostpunk 2 you are the "president" of a colony situated in a frozen wasteland. The main selling point of this game is that your people are very politically passionate and you have to control a council that votes on policies. Want to conserve some coal and pass austerity measures? First you need to earn the favor of industrialists by either passing laws they want or just buying them off, so that you can get enough votes to pass the bill you actually want to pass. In addition to that you manage heat, coal, food, and other resources as you explore the frost and establish supply lines. Frostpunk 1 was amazing at making your people feel like actual people. In that game you were the "captain" so nobody could oppose you in passing laws. But every law you passed you felt responsible. Every life the frost took from you was painful. I was hoping for more of the same from FP2 but they made the laws matter less than the first game and overall it seems they rushed the production so the game was overall hard to parse and confusing to play. I do hope they will make it better in time, but for now, I recommend playing Frostpunk 1 instead. The council system is super interesting nevertheless. So interesting that in the next game jam I do I'll try to make my own council system because obviously I Can Do It Better What Were They Thinking™.

Aaand that was the first part! I'll post part 2 next week. This collection was a bit less straightforward but I hope you liked reading about my thoughts about these games!
