It's a Friday, time for another **Games I've Played Recently**! Every time I keep thinking "oh I haven't played that many games since last time" then I check and oh look at that I have enough to make 3 more of these. Soooo, **Part 1**:

- **Lost Nova:** Cute little (~2h) indie relaxing exploration game. In Lost Nova your ship crashes on a planet with lovely aliens. You then collect materials to repair your ship and help the locals, all the while slowly figuring out why the planet is all cracked open. My brain always enjoys some "collect 10 crystal to unlock jumping boots" sort of games, so I enjoyed my time with it. And the story was surprisingly nice too (hint: it's about stress lol) so that's a bonus. Nice way to spend an evening or two.
- **Mewgenics:** Latest game from the makers of Binding of Isaac. In Mewgenics you gather some stray cats then send them to battle weird creatures (there is a lot of poop jokes involved). This has become a small phenomenon, and I did enjoy it, but not enough to finish it <img class="slack-emoji" src="/images/slack-stuck_out_tongue-D29C10FD82C7.png" alt=":stuck_out_tongue:" />. The cat combat is nice with a lot of synergies and strategy, and building a house for the cats then breeding them (because, mewgenics), but I think some aspects of it work weird? It's a bit too RNG heavy for my taste (a run can get ruined by a single unlucky event. This game is HARSH) and the metagame of cat breeding and house-making was a bit too slow to keep me going after unlocking like zone 3. I would've loved this back when I was a teen and had a lot more free time on my hands though. I played for ~25h, but apparently it takes more like 80h to finish it. But it was fun before the grinding kicked in! Give it a go if you liked Isaac. Don't be fooled by the kitty exterior though.
- **It Takes a War:** Yet another indie game, this one is super short at <1h. In It Takes a War you are placed in an "online" lobby of a friend group playing a CS like shooter game, reminiscent of 2000s online lobby gameplay. Then weird things start happening and you see this friend group fall apart in real time. I liked this one, because both the story was good and it did some nice meta-horror (that I don't want to spoil, but play in a room alone with headphones in for best experience). Ofc the best part of it was how it sorta kinda resembled how it was feeling for me when I was playing shooter games with my 5-stack friend group. Give it a go if you want a more "artistic" game experience (no shooter skills needed!)
- **Hades II:** I was looking for the full release of this because I enjoyed Hades 1 a lot, but I was also skeptical. Hades 2 continues to follow the Hades 1 formula of hack and slash with a story about Greek Gods doing their thing. I played the original Hades 1 up to its main story conclusion, but I kinda dropped off from this one. Hades 2 focuses and expands the gameplay from Hades 1, and there is still plenty of story around. I played Hades 1 for its story while enjoying the gameplay, but in Hades 2 the story never worked for me. Hades 1 was simple, Zagreus wants out of hell, and Hades is both your dad and there to stop you. It worked great because it's a silly premise and none of the characters take it too seriously (Zagreus saying "back again" lives rent free in my head) and you just wanted OUT. Meanwhile in Hades 2 there is Chronos? And you want to go to Hell? And you have your mentor instead of Hades, but she still wants you? And they want you out of there, for reasons? With all that said, if you LOVED the gameplay from Hades 1, then there is much more of it with a lot more deep strategy in Hades 2. It just feels more like DLC to me than a full game (and I think it started as a DLC too).

And that's all for this time! Some unfortunate sequel misses for me, but they are still highly acclaimed games, so who am I to speak? Have a nice weekend everyone.
