After another insanely long gap (wow 6 months already?) it's time for some **Games I've Played Recently**. I'll split this one into parts as well, soooo **Part 1**:

- **Half Life 2:** When I wrote the last one of these half life 2 had just released a 20th anniversary update soo I replayed up until episode 2 (maybe I'll play that one too?). Half Life is definitely still a great game, and it was interesting to play it now that I'm an adult and see everything I missed as a child. The dev commentary was great to listen to as well. I kinda dropped it after episode 1 because it was getting too samey, but thats still a lot of game!
- **Sixty Four:** I love my incremental games with strategy and "story" and this one is up there. In Sixty Four you are in a white void and you are mining cubes that you need to make your mining more efficient. The nice thing about this one is that the deeper you go the different cubes you get, and different cubes meaningfully change how you optimize your mining build. The pacing is a bit weird, but it was nice to just run in the background and check back every now and then to optimize my mining structures.
- **Diceomancer:** An interesting roguelike deckbuilder (like Slay the Spire) where you have a magic die that can change -any- number in the game. Enemy has too much health? just roll a die to set it to 2. Card dealing too little damage? roll a die to set it to 6. This game both works as a deck builder, but also a puzzle game. There were a bunch of "aha" moments when playing where I figured out a totally different way to break the game with the magic die. The card play isn't as good as the other ones in the genre, but this game more than makes up for it with it's unique reality bending die.
- **A Game About Digging A Hole:** This game went viral a while back and it was interesting to play for me because I was following the developer in twitter. They were working on a much more complex game, but one day they posted a tweet that was like "you know what, I'll just take a break and make this little game". I'm pretty sure the digging game sold a lot more than what they expected from even their original proper game. The game itself is super simple, yet super satisfying. You have a tiny shovel, and you dig the voxel ground. Quickly you start to strategize on how best to dig so you can climb back, and how to make the most money with each trip. This is a short game, but a lot of fun figuring things out! And the simple act of digging is a lot of fun too.

And that's for Part 1! You'll have to wait for a week until Part 2. And who knows, I've made some reminders for myself so maybe next time I won't forget to write these for so long. Happy rest of your week!
