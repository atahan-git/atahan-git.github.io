After the long long pause, the New Games I've Played is here again! Last year I was posting this only in [one internal work] chat but I found out about [another one] so I will also post it there <img class="slack-emoji" src="/images/slack-dance-1C2AB6FB8E77.gif" alt=":dance:" />. I was super busy this week with my ramp up, so I didn't have time to play anything new. But I'm just going to list games I've played in the past couple of weeks:

- **Disco Elysium:** This is a weird one. I heard about how good this one is for a long time, and finally after wanting to watch a good youtube video about it (I had to play before the video spoiled it!) I played this. Disco Elysium is this narrative game where you are a cop who lost his memory and is trying to solve a murder, while talking to parts of your inner psyche and considering societal issues. Great dialog, lots of interesting topics handled, and this game also gives you lots of chances to pick all sorts of weird dialog options as well. I still haven't finished it, and I might not finish it this time around. Disco Elysium is one of those games that hits very hard if you are in a particular period in your life (like Outer Wilds), but it is hard to get into if you are not feeling that certain way. Still totally recommended!
- **Ixion:** I played this game last year, and it was super fun! This is a brutal spaceship colony builder with survival at its core, similar to Frostpunk. Recently they released a difficulty update so I had to replay it on the hardest difficulty. And it is still super fun!
- **Trailmakers:** This is a chill, vehicle builder game using blocks. I had a lot of fun with this one, especially with a podcast/video running on the side. The game has a couple of different modes but the main one is where you have to navigate difficult terrain and move very heavy "ores" (usually spheres) to collection points. You need to climb mountains, slippery surfaces, go in and out of water, and later on even fly.
- **Phoenix Hope:** Earlier this summer Kingdom Eighties was released, and that was super fun, so I was searching for similar games and I found this. Kingdom Eighties is a 2d game where you are a "monarch" who runs around to collect coins, then use those to build a simple kingdom, and you get attacked from both sides so you need archers and walls. Phoenix Hope is similar, but you don't exist in the world and just build a kingdom. It is still in early access, so the game ends after the first boss, but even that was super challenging and nice to figure out.

And that is all for now! Hopefully this week will be slightly more chill, giving me more headspace to try out new games.
