I totally got busy and skipped some weeks there but it continues, Part 3 of Games I've Played Recently is here! This one is all weird games:

- **How Fish is Made:** This is a short and free game about... life I guess? Flop around as a fish. Talk to other fish. Decide whether you will go UP or you if would rather go DOWN. I had played this one a long time ago, but recently I watched a video called Gross Games about Flesh and Stuff on youtube that mentioned this game so I had to play it again. If you have about 20 minutes of free time and want some weirdness go play this one!
- **The Perfect Vermin:** Also a short free game about... breaking furniture? This one is also from the video Gross Games about Flesh and Stuff so that should give you an idea. Why is the furniture all fleshy inside? Play and find out! This one I liked the mechanics a lot more than story. It's a lot of fun to smash things with a hammer and watch them explode.
- **Mouthwashing:** This one is made by the same people who made How Fish is Made! And that's how I found about it. Go on a journey with your fellow underpaid workers on an interplanetary cargo ship. Fight your mental demons. Figure out if anyone survives the trip when things go wrong. This is a short one too, and I finished it in about 5 hours. That was definitely a weird weekend. Games can be so weird. Expect an experience like watching an experimental artsy movie.
- **1000x Resist:** This is a weird game too, but for different reasons. 1000x Resist starts at a little garden-dormitory place where everyone is clones of this one girl. Humanity is wiped out by a disease, and only this girl is immune. Walk around in this beautiful place that I definitely would love to live in, talk to your clones doing their tasks, dive into memories about teenage school life troubles, see how the Hong Kong events affected some of the locals... Great weird game that tries to do sooo many different things, and somehow does a decent job at all of them.
- **Receiver 2:** Yep, this one is a weird one too. Familiarize yourself with how to operate various handguns. Make sure to safely holster them so that you don't shoot yourself in the leg. Carefully aim at the weak spots of various turrets and drones. All the while learning more about a "Threat" that is mindwashing people while you resist it's temptations. And hey it's also about social media. But be warned, this game is pretty brutal in it's difficulty so you will die a lot.

Aaand that was the Part 3. I've almost finished all the games I've stored up, so it's almost time to play some new games? Don't worry I'm already in the process of playing through a couple so I'm sure I'll have the next batch after these soon enough. But until then, Happy rest of your week!
