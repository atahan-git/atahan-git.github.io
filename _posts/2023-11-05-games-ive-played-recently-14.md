After a somewhat long gap I finally played enough new games for another New Games I've Played Last Month or so:

- **Orb of Creation:** This is a pretty fun incremental/idle/puzzle game. You get mana and then you have to combine and cast spells to create increasingly arcane resources. Although it does become unmanageable later on, the early game is super nice and fun. I wanted to play more of the same of this so I checked out a couple other similar games and also enjoyed The First Alkohistorian which is a nice free game with fairly simple mechanics and an interesting enough story. Although it also does get unmanageable as the stages progress.
- **DYSMANYLE:** A nice relaxing game about, well, dismantling the world. You are in a weird magic apocalypse scenario and pretty much everything in the world is destructible, from furniture to walls to cars. This one plays like an incremental game too as you go around collecting scraps to upgrade your wrench to dismantle even sturdier things to get even more scraps. Really nice to play while listening to some podcast in the background.
- **The Gap:** A nice short story about memory loss, dealing with an incurable disease, and parallel universes. The graphics and effects in this game are gorgeous! There are also some light philosophical and practical questions here and there too with some nice "aha!" puzzle moments.

I did actually play a lot more other games but I didn't particularly like any of the other ones enough to share. Luckily Steam's 2 hour refund window is pretty generous to try out new games! I just need to be really disciplined to only buy games if I'm going to immediately play them, or else missing the 2 week deadline is super easy (and I do have waaay too many games just sitting there, bought from sales, never played...). I'm also playing through another Factorio mod, Nullius, and that's been a lot of fun, but I will write about that properly once I finish it. And that's all for now!
