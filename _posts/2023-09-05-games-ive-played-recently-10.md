I finally played some new games! I'm still playing Baldur's Gate and I finally reached Act 3 and hopefully sometime soon I can finish that and write about it lol. I will probably replay it though. Sooooo the New Games I've Played:

- **Factorio: Freight Forwarding Mod:** Last week Factorio finally made a blog post about more details about their upcoming DLC and it looks lit! With that motivation I checked their mod portal and this mod caught my eye. Factorio is, as the name suggests, a factory builder game (they have [a really good trailer](https://youtu.be/J8SBp4SyvLc?feature=shared), go watch it). And its one of my favorite games that I go back to every now and then and play some new mods, which often add many hours of content and completely change the game. Freight Forwarding changes the logistics of the game by putting a lot of things farther apart and puts everything on islands. It works fine, but I stopped halfway through the mod because its main mechanic of putting stuff in boxes before shipping is very fiddly to set up with Factorio's mechanics. I do like the idea though and maybe with some of the train changes the Factorio DLC was talking about these kinds of mods will become better!
- **Last Call BBS:** As the name suggests this is the last game of Zachtronics, a game studio that makes all sorts of puzzle games that created a whole "zach-like" genre. This game is a collection of 8 smaller games and like other Zachtronics games they are a hit or miss based on what flavor of puzzles you like. I really liked the meaty cell based puzzler X`BPGH and the circuit builder. The solitaire version included is also surprisingly good and I played waaay too much of that. I will even adopt its rules for when I play solitaire with real cards lol. It is sad to see Zachtronics go, but I guess its nice that they are going out with a bang instead of slowly decaying into obscurity. Their legacy will hopefully live on, proving a template on how to build puzzle games for future developers.
