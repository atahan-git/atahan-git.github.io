Another week another New Games I've Played:

- **Baldur's Gate 3:** I've only scratched the surface of this one as it is famously a very big game! I managed to clear out most of the first area though. Baldur's Gate is best described as a D&D like game where you have a party of heroes and you go around trying to figure out the main quest (you have a parasite in your brain that will turn you into a monster) while doing lots of side quests. I really like how every story bit hints at multiple ways to solve the problem. And also the game doesn't really judge you for picking any of the options, there is no morality meter here. It even rewards you if you have an "evil" character in your party and you make that character act evil. The combat is also pretty fun to figure out, giving you enough options to combo attacks (eg create a water puddle first, then use a lightning spell for extra damage) while also having enough simple damage spells so that you are not forced to combo just to play. Overall plenty of fun if you have lots of hours to sink into a long adventure.
- **Wall World: Deep Threat DLC:** I played this game a whole bunch when it was first released, so I was excited for the DLC. In Wall World you live on a mysterious wall, and you go around the wall with a robo-spider, digging mines in the wall and upgrading, all the while dealing with a leviathan monster that keeps attacking you. For a roguelike game like this (a big inspiration for this game was Dome Keeper) it has a very interesting story. Like I was genuinely interested in finding the edge of the wall as the main character was also interested in it. I was very curious about how they were going to handle the DLC because in the base game you have a lot of permanent upgrades that make you overpowered by the end of the game, and I was worried they would take all that away for the DLC. Luckily they didn't, and instead they added more upgrades! And also changed a few items here and there so that it feels fresh enough, while also letting you keep most of your powers from the last game. And they hint for yet another chapter in the story, which makes it all the more exciting. I just wish that the DLC was as long as the base game, but a nice and short expansion works better than it being overly long for this sort of game.
