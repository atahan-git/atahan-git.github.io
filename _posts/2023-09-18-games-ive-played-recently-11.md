After a long pause due to Baldur's Gate 3, I finally have another **New Games I've Played**.

- **Baldur's Gate 3:** Pretty good game overall and everybody knows about it so I won't repeat much. It took me 74 hours to complete. The last act did feel a bit too long, especially because I reached the maximum level 12 pretty early so there were a lot of just going around and completing the remaining interesting missions around. I do want to replay this game sometime though, even with everything I think I missed a lot of content. Definitely after a break though lol.
- **Crop Rotation:** A tiny little automated deck builder-ish game. In this game you pick out plants with various bonuses and requirements, then they automatically and randomly get planted on a grid. Based on their positioning and your various upgrades you earn money to buy more plants. Nice little indie game, although it definitely lasted waaay too long. They could make each playthrough half as long and it would be much more fun. Especially because later in the game you have soo many plants it gets harder to make meaningful choices.
- **Heretic's Fork:** Another fairly small indie game. I played this way before when they had a demo, and they finally released it. In this game you get cards to build turrets or barracks which then "keep souls from escaping hell". You get a certain amount of mana each turn and you can also merge cards instead of playing them, after which you get a skill check in the form of a wave of enemies. Pretty interesting spin on deck building and tower defense.
- **Bread & Fred:** A super cute game that I was playing with my girlfriend. In this one you are two penguins tied by a rope and you are trying to climb a mountain. The rope tying you two is pretty short, so you have to work together to jump around and use the rope to do some pretty difficult platforming. This is also a "frustration" game where a small mistake might make you fall down a big distance. But they include checkpoints as an "accessibility" feature and I definitely recommend playing with that. Lots of fun with "1, 2, 3, jump!"

And that's all for this week. Lots of fun. Maybe I will play Starfield sometime but that is also a multi week game like BG3 lol.
