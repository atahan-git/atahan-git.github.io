Ok here's part 3 of Games I've Played Recently:

- **Slay the Spire 2:** I played the first game for 100+ hours, and I have also played the second one about the same. Slay the Spire was a genre creating roguelike deckbuilder where you defeat monsters using cards you play while building a strong deck to defeat the boss at the top. StS2 does more of the same. They made some subtle changes to the energy economy which I think works well, and the ancients add great flavor to different runs. StS1 had a crazy 20 "ascensions" per character, where every time you beat the game with one ascension you unlock a harder one. StS2 has only 10 per character, and it was fun to beat ascension 10 with all characters. The ascensions are also a bit easier compared to StS1, where I never beat all 20. I also felt StS2 was "easier" to guess winning strategies, and it was more fun for me to slowly improve my win rate. That might also be because I'm now a lot better at games compared to when I was first playing StS1, and that StS is one of the games where I watch random youtube videos about for strategy and game design. It was a lot of fun to play this one right at release as all the existing StS community was playing it and making videos about it. My Youtube Shorts feed was 50% StS 2 content for a couple of weeks as I climbed the ascensions lol. StS2 is still in Early Access and doesn't have the final boss yet. I'm excited for what they will make for the final boss, and will probably play again to beat it.
- **The Alters: Last Variable DLC:** The Alters was one of my favorite games from 2025, and it was exciting to learn about the DLC. In The Alters, you are Jan Dolski. You find yourself in the now standard "uncaring corporation sends a bunch of people on a mission to mine some materials on an alien planet" plot. The main thing in this one is that your mission is equipped with a mysterious Quantum Computer and the computer chose the seemingly least talented crew member, you, to survive. Oh and you can create "alters" of yourself, what if Jan didn't quit college in his twenties? He becomes Jan Scientist and now super smart! You then go around trying to escape this planet that has a bunch of other mysteries, while your alters and you fight about things because even though you are the same guy you all have very different world views. In the DLC its more of the same, where (minor spoiler) Jan Scientist stays behind on the planet to study it. The DLC felt weaker than the main story, but still it had solid gameplay. I loved both the story and the gameplay of the main game, and played it a couple of times to get the different endings. With the DLC I played it once, mostly for the gameplay, but it was still a pretty good experience. The DLC isn't a Game of the Year contender like the main game was, but if you play and like the main game then it's more of the good stuff!
- **Super Battle Golf:** After so long wanting to try one of the "friendslop" games I finally got a chance to play one. Well to be fair this is more of a "battle with your friends" sub-type of the friendslop than the "do missions" one, but still. Super Battle Golf is basically golf but Mario Kart. You all scramble to get your ball in the hole while collecting powerups to mess with other people. I played this literally one time for about 3 hours and it was a lot of fun! But I do feel I experienced most of what the game has to offer <img class="slack-emoji" src="/images/slack-stuck_out_tongue-D29C10FD82C7.png" alt=":stuck_out_tongue:" />. But still, it was fun to learn the ropes and keep shouting "target [friend's name] always" as one of us was a lot better than the rest and if everyone didn't target him he would win every time. But alas, the stars never aligned again and we never managed to pull the 10-20 people in the extended friend group or even just a handful to play again... Logistics is hard.
- **Meccha Chameleon:** Yet another friendslop game, and a mega viral one at that. In Meccha Chameleon you are a little white dude. You get a little bit of time to find a good spot in the level and you need to paint yourself to hide from the hunters when they arrive. A small tweak to the "prop hunt" gamemode that existed for many years, but such a great twist. This was a LOT of fun! I was playing this with a bunch of people from a gamejam I did this summer and it was great to chat with them again. The artists amazed us with their amazing painting skills, while the rest showed creativity on how well you can hide with just some blobs of color. The highlight was in the painting gallery level (the most popular custom map) where 6 of us were looking at the same painting trying to find the last person, and we could not find him until the timer ran out, for a whole minute. The game has a fun "whistle" mechanic so we all knew which painting it was, and just as a comparison you usually find people in like 5 seconds. But alas, stars only aligned one time, and also just like many in this friendslop genre nobody had much motivation to play again after one time. Fun while it lasts.

And that's all I have for now. I'm sure in a couple more months I'll have another big list of games and wonder when I played them all... Happy weekend everyone!
