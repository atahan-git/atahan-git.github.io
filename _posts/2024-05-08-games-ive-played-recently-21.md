Aaand here's the part 2 of The Games I've Played Last Month-ish:

- **Dredge:** This was a game that surprised me. In this game you are a fisher doing fishing in a collection of islands where some weird stuff is happening. You go around collecting fish via various minigames, slot them on the square grid of your always too small ship inventory, sell them, and do quests. But at night time various unholy creatures cause you trouble but you also catch aberrated fish that sell for more! Its always thrilling to catch just a few more fish but then you are a little too far from the docks so you have to make a run for it to not loose your loot to some enormous creature lurking below. Totally recommended if you like slowly upgrading your gear while running around a nicely designed open world doing quests for quirky npcs. Then ending has a nice plot twist too!
- **Rusty's Retirement:** I was seeing screenshots of this game for a bit on twitter before it got released so I was excited for this little indie game. In this game you get a little farm covering bottom 1/3 of your screen. Plant some crops, make some money, buy robots to automate the labor, make some biofuel, buy more expensive plants, decorate your farm, and so on. I liked this one because you can finish it fairly quickly (like in 1-3 days if you keep it running and look at it every now and then) and its very charming to just look at your farm while doing other things.
- **King of the Bridge:** In this tiny (1-2 hours to beat it) indie game you try to cross a bridge occupied by a troll that will let you pass if you beat him in "chess". But the rules are a lot different than regular chess so in the first 1/3 of the game you basically play to find the rules. Oh and also cheating is allowed! You have to catch your opponent cheating by cross referencing the in-game rule book and get to break the rules yourself. This one has a nice plot twist "bad ending" and an even more interesting and involved "good ending"! a couple of fun easter eggs here and there too.

And that's all for now. Maybe next time I'll remember to write this before collecting so many games lol.
