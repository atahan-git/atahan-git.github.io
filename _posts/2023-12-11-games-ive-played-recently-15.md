It's been a long time since last I've made one of these because I haven't found any new game worth sharing in a bit, but also I've been a little too into Factorio (I'm past 1k hours at this point lol) which didn't leave time for any other games. But anyways, here's another one of those New Games I've Played Sometime Recently:

- **Slay the Princess:** This is a very weird game. Imagine the Stanley Parable merged with a visual novel and sprinkle in some great and horrific at times art. I liked this one because it's fairly short and sweet. It brings a unique twist to the "multiple endings" genre, (minor spoiler) where it actually stops you from trying out every possible ending. Forcing you to stop after you get your unique mix of endings. It was an interesting journey for sure, but also this is definitely not a game if you don't like choices and meta narrative much.
- **Factorio Nullius Mod:** I think I spent over 200 hours in the past couple weeks and months on this alone. Factorio is a great game on its own but mods definitely take it to a different level and this mod changes soo much and a lot of it is in the best way. Nullius is a mod that is set "pre-factorio" where the planet lacks life (and hostile biters) and you are a von-neumann probe with a mission to make more of yourself and seed life. Without life there is no coal and oil so you make most of your stuff using air, water, and some existing minerals around. I loved how the player really feels like a customizable robot because this mod adds soo many attachments that now I miss in other mods and the base game. The mod also adds unique new challenges like weird energy generation and storage methods, and lots and lots of byproducts. One of the main things is that you create hydrogen chloride and caustic solution through the same process, and lots of production chains either use one of the two or generate it as a byproduct and you have to balance it. Caustic can just be vented into the oceans, but you need to neutralize hydrogen chloride before you can be rid of it so there are some unique logistical challenges not found in any other mod. Although this mod is definitely a bit too long. The science needs ramp up quite a lot mid game and once you "solve" the byproduct challenge with trains or such, late game just becomes setting up 20 different trains for each ingredient late game requires. I didn't finish this but I will definitely come back to it in the future.
- **Factorio Exotic Industries Mod:** Halfway through Nullius I got too overwhelmed but I still wanted to keep playing so I took a break and played this mod instead lol. This mod also shakes things up slightly with some light byproducts that you can very easily get rid of, and some interesting upgrades you can get to your smelting. It also has an interesting tech scaling where things get more expensive with each tech. Although I think it was scaling too fast, and the options to tune it were a little too insufficient. The mod adds some giant inserters though and I was very sad they didn't include it as a separate mod hehe. After playing this for a while it suffered from the same problem of just having more of the same so I quit this one halfway through and actually returned to Nullius, then didn't finish it the second time either.

And that's all! I'm actually playing Dyson Sphere Program now, which is another fairly long factory game. I'm enjoying it so far and definitely will write about it once I'm done. Hopefully I will find some more nice contained games to write about soon. Ideally some games that don't take 200 hours to play, but those are the best ones!
