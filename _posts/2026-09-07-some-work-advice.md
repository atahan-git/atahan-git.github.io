If you haven't read it yet, please take a look at [My Thoughts on Advice]() before reading any of my advice articles!

1. document everything, summary and lots and lots of random references, WITH DATES
2. over estimate time needed, do not overwork if you dont want to. say "they might do it faster, if you want me this is the time"
3. when competing stuff is there, make them talk with each other to figure it out. Say "can do it, but these things will be late"
4. when delivering decisions go simple,
one paragraph summary, what is the problem, 3 solutions with time estimate pros and cons, recommended solution, attached long explanation after the executive summary

5. make internal note on what will make you quit, if these dont happen, look around. look around anyways
